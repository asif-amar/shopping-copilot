# Privacy Policy for Shopping Copilot

**Last Updated:** September 19, 2025

## Overview

Shopping Copilot ("we," "our," or "the extension") is a Chrome browser extension that provides AI-powered shopping assistance for Israeli grocery websites. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

### Authentication Information
- **Google Account Information**: When you sign in, we collect your Google email address and profile information through Google OAuth
- **Authentication Tokens**: We store authentication tokens to maintain your login session

### Website Content and Shopping Data
- **Product Information**: We access product names, prices, and descriptions from supported shopping websites (Rami Levy, Shufersal)
- **Cart Data**: We access your shopping cart contents to provide cart management assistance
- **Website Cookies**: We access cookies from shopping websites necessary for authentication and cart functionality

### Conversation Data
- **Chat History**: We store your conversations with the AI assistant to provide continuity across sessions
- **Shopping Preferences**: We may store your shopping preferences to provide personalized recommendations

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:
- Provide AI-powered shopping assistance and recommendations
- Manage your shopping cart across supported websites
- Search for products and compare prices
- Maintain conversation history for better user experience
- Authenticate you with shopping websites and our service

## Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage**: Authentication tokens and conversation history are stored locally in your browser
- **Secure Transmission**: All data transmitted to our servers is encrypted using HTTPS
- **No Third-Party Sharing**: We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes

## Supported Websites

This extension operates on the following websites:
- Rami Levy (www.rami-levy.co.il)
- Shufersal (www.shufersal.co.il, online.shufersal.co.il)

## Third-Party Services

We integrate with:
- **Google OAuth**: For user authentication (governed by Google's Privacy Policy)
- **Our Backend Service**: For AI shopping assistance (hosted on Render.com)

## Data Retention

- **Conversation History**: Stored locally until you clear browser data or uninstall the extension
- **Authentication Data**: Cleared when you log out or revoke permissions

## Your Rights
